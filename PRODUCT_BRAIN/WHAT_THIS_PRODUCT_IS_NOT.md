# WHAT THIS PRODUCT IS NOT
## Explicit Boundaries to Prevent Conceptual Drift

**Purpose**: Define what this product will NEVER become. LLMs and feature creep constantly push toward generic patterns. This document blocks that drift.

---

## THIS IS NOT A GENERIC STOCK SCREENER

**Why it's not**:
- Generic screeners show static snapshots of stocks
- They present data without context or narrative
- They optimize for quantity over quality
- They treat all stocks as equal regardless of regime

**What we do instead**:
- Detect transitions and momentum shifts
- Provide contextual narrative around setups
- Maintain scarcity - only quality setups shown
- Regime-aware filtering and ranking

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- Giant tables with 50+ columns
- "All stocks matching X criteria" lists
- Static filters without temporal context
- Equal visual weight for all results

---

## THIS IS NOT AN ANALYTICS DASHBOARD

**Why it's not**:
- Dashboards present data for exploration
- They encourage analysis paralysis
- They prioritize information density over actionability
- They're designed for discovery, not operations

**What we do instead**:
- Compress context into actionable signals
- Prioritize operational scanning
- Reduce cognitive load through hierarchy
- Design for decision-making, not exploration

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- Multiple panels with charts everywhere
- Endless metrics and KPIs
- Drill-down interfaces
- "Explore the data" workflows

---

## THIS IS NOT A SAAS PLATFORM WITH STARTUP AESTHETICS

**Why it's not**:
- SaaS platforms optimize for onboarding and feature breadth
- Startup aesthetics prioritize decoration over information
- They use bright colors, gradients, and playful design
- They emphasize feature lists over operational depth

**What we do instead**:
- Institutional, professional aesthetics
- Low saturation, high information density
- Minimal decoration, maximum signal
- Operational depth over feature breadth

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- Bright, saturated color schemes
- Gradient backgrounds and buttons
- Playful illustrations and icons
- "Feature-first" marketing language
- Onboarding tours and tooltips
- Gamification elements

---

## THIS IS NOT AN AI PREDICTION SYSTEM

**Why it's not**:
- Prediction systems claim to forecast price movements
- They rely on black-box ML models
- They promise certainty where none exists
- They create dependency on opaque systems

**What we do instead**:
- Rule-based, interpretable systems
- Focus on pattern recognition, not prediction
- Provide context for discretionary decisions
- Maintain human control and oversight

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- "AI predicts stock will go up" messaging
- Black-box recommendation engines
- Confidence scores without explanation
- Automated trading suggestions
- Machine learning as a selling point

---

**Why it's not**:
- Social platforms rely on crowd sentiment
- They optimize for engagement, not edge
- They create herding behavior
- They prioritize popularity over quality

**What we do instead**:
- Institutional sponsorship tracking
- Focus on smart money flows
- Independent signal generation
- Quality over popularity

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- "Most popular stocks" lists
- Social sentiment indicators
- Copy trading features
- Community leaderboards
- User-generated content feeds

---

## THIS IS NOT A RETAIL GIMMICK PLATFORM

**Why it's not**:
- Retail gimmicks include alerts, signals, hot picks
- They optimize for excitement, not edge
- They create dependency and FOMO
- They promise easy money

**What we do instead**:
- Professional-grade tools
- Focus on process, not picks
- Enable discretionary trading
- Require skill and judgment

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- "Hot stock alerts" notifications
- "Buy now" buttons
- Get-rich-quick marketing
- Simplified interfaces that hide complexity
- One-click trading suggestions

---

## THIS IS NOT A FEATURE-HEAVY PRODUCT

**Why it's not**:
- Feature-heavy products become maintenance burdens
- They confuse users with options
- They dilute focus and philosophy
- They create technical debt

**What we do instead**:
- Surgical, focused features
- Each feature earns its place
- Maintain operational clarity
- Prioritize workflow continuity

**Anti-pattern to avoid**:
- Long feature checklists
- "Me too" feature additions
- Options, settings, and toggles everywhere
- Feature bloat from user requests
- Competitor feature parity

## THE LLM DRIFT TEST

**If an LLM suggests any of the following, it has drifted**:

❌ "Let's add a social sentiment feature"
❌ "Users want to see more charts"
❌ "We should add AI predictions"
❌ "A real-time alert system would be great"
❌ "Let's make it more colorful and engaging"
❌ "Users should be able to customize everything"
❌ "We need more features to compete"
❌ "A backtesting module would add value"
❌ "Let's add a news feed"
❌ "Users want to track their portfolio"

**Correct LLM response**:
"This violates WHAT_THIS_PRODUCT_IS_NOT.md. Suggesting this feature would drift us toward [anti-pattern]. Instead, consider [aligned alternative]."

## BOUNDARY ENFORCEMENT

**When evaluating any feature**:
1. Check against this document
2. If it matches any "NOT" category → Reject
3. If it's unclear → Consult DECISION_FILTER.md
4. Document the rejection rationale

**When LLMs drift**:
1. Cite this document explicitly
2. Reference the specific anti-pattern
3. Redirect to aligned alternatives
4. Update LLM_INTERACTION_RULES.md if needed

## PHILOSOPHY BEHIND BOUNDARIES

**Why boundaries matter**:
- Focus requires exclusion
- Edge requires specialization
- Clarity requires simplification
- Identity requires definition

**The institutional edge comes from**:
- Doing one thing exceptionally well
- Avoiding retail distractions
- Maintaining professional standards
- Preserving operational philosophy

**Without boundaries**:
- Product becomes generic screener
- Visual identity becomes SaaS startup
- Philosophy becomes diluted
- Edge is lost to feature creep

3. Ask: "Does this maintain scarcity and quality?"
4. Ask: "Does this preserve transition-first thinking?"
