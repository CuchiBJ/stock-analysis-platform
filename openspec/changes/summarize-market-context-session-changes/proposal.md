## Why
Operators need changes since the preceding session with deterioration first and inspectable evidence. This extends transitions over snapshots and interpretability.
## What Changes
- Add a dedicated-page summary covering participation, leadership, posture, SPY, QQQ and VIX.
- Expose dated previous benchmark and VIX closes, requiring aligned dates.
- Disclose unavailable history explicitly.
## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `market-context-session-changes`: deterministic dated changes.
### Modified Capabilities
None.
## Non-goals
No posture reconstruction, persistence, scoring/policy or dashboard changes, divergences, sector evolution, deployments or backfills.
## Impact
Additive benchmark/volatility fields and dedicated frontend module/component/tests. No dependencies or migrations. Comparison logic is isolated rather than extending the large engine.
